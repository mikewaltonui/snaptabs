SnapTabs privacy policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

SnapTabs — Open Multiple URLs is a Chrome extension that opens a list of web addresses in browser tabs. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data about you.

What is stored

SnapTabs saves the following using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API, which writes to your own computer:

  • The list of addresses currently in the text box, if "Remember this list" is on
  • Your option settings, such as pacing, filters, and tab group name
  • Any lists you choose to name and save

This data never leaves your device. It is not synced to a Google account, not backed up anywhere, and not readable by anyone but you. Removing the extension deletes all of it. You can also clear it at any time with the Clear button and by deleting your saved lists.

What is not collected

SnapTabs makes no network requests. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party code of any kind. It does not read your browsing history, your open tabs, your bookmarks, or the content of any page you visit.

Permissions and why they exist

  • Storage — saves your list and settings on your own machine.
  • Tabs — creates the tabs for the addresses you entered, and unloads them if you turn on the memory-saving option. It does not read existing tabs.
  • Tab groups — names and colours the group your new tabs land in, when you ask for one.
  • Search — opens your default search engine for lines that are not valid addresses, only if you turn that option on. It is off by default.

Children

SnapTabs is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
